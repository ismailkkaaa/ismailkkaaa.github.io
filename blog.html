Graphic Design Workflow

Graphic design is not just about making visuals. It is about communication. Every design should convey a message clearly and attract attention.

I create posters and social media designs using tools like Photoshop and Canva. My focus is on color balance, typography, and clean layout.

A good design should be simple, modern, and effective.

Video Editing and Reels

Video editing plays a huge role in modern content creation. Short-form videos like reels need to be fast, engaging, and visually appealing.

I use tools like Premiere Pro and CapCut to edit videos with smooth transitions and effects. My goal is to create videos that keep viewers engaged.

Editing is not just about cuts, but about storytelling.

Photo Editing Style

Photo editing helps enhance visuals and create a strong impact. I focus on color grading, lighting, and clarity.

Using tools like Photoshop and Lightroom, I create clean and professional edits.
